// About the fest

One current. Every direction.

NeoSpark '2.0 is the annual technical and creative symposium hosted by the Department of Computer Science and Applications at Jeppiaar College of Arts and Science.

Every circuit needs a spark to move — this fest is built the same way. Solo challenges test how sharp you think alone, and team events test how well that thinking travels between people.

Whether you build, argue, design, or perform: there's a track wired for you. Show up, plug in, and let the current carry you to the next round.
